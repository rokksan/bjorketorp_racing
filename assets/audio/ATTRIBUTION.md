## Combat effects (CC0 1.0)

- `sfx/shotgun.wav`, `pistol.wav`, `rifle.wav`: Tabasco, [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds). Sources: shotty.wav, cz.wav, mosin.wav.
- `sfx/shell.wav`, `rack.wav`, `chamber.wav`: zer0_sol, [Shotgun Reload Sound Effects](https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects). Sources: First Shell, Rack, Shell in Chamber.
- `sfx/cluck-1.wav` through `cluck-3.wav`: Breviceps, [Chicken clucking](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/456803/), public HQ MP3 preview.

All above are published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Edited into short mono 22.05 kHz WAV clips, high-pass filtered and faded at the edges. Exact source selections are documented in `sfx/edits.json`. No original multi-take recordings ship with the game.

`potato.wav` derives from the credited Tabasco shotgun clip (lowered sample rate, 650 Hz low-pass, fade and gain). `potato-hit.wav` derives from zer0_sol's chamber clip (lowered sample rate, 900 Hz low-pass and fade). Both remain CC0.
